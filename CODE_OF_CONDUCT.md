# Code of Conduct

AKI RAG Middleware is a small technical open-source project. Participation should make the project easier to use, understand and improve.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical ideas and disagreements on their merits.
- Be precise, constructive and respectful of other participants' time.
- Assume that deployment environments, experience levels and constraints differ.
- Welcome bug reports, questions and corrections made in good faith.
- Protect private data: do not ask people to publish credentials, private corpus contents or other sensitive information merely to obtain support.
- Give credit for other people's work and preserve license/copyright notices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, publication of secrets/private data, spam and sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Moderation should be proportionate and should distinguish ordinary technical disagreement from abusive conduct.

For a sensitive conduct matter that should not be discussed publicly, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel without disclosing sensitive details, unless a private project contact has already been published.

This Code of Conduct governs participation in the upstream project. It does not alter the software license or anyone's rights under the AGPL.
