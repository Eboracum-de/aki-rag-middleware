This Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") applies to contributions accepted into the upstream **SunaQ** repository maintained by **Eboracum GmbH** ("Project Steward").

The purpose of this Agreement is narrow: contributors keep ownership of their work, while the Project Steward receives enough additional rights to maintain the public AGPL project and, if there is demand, offer the same combined code under separate commercial terms.

This Agreement is **not** a copyright assignment.

## 4. Public open-source commitment

For every Contribution accepted under this Agreement, the Project Steward commits that the accepted Contribution, as incorporated in the public upstream project, will remain available from the Project Steward under **GNU AGPL-3.0-only**.

The Project Steward is not required to accept or keep any Contribution. Contributions are provided without an obligation by either side to provide support, maintenance, warranty or compensation, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## 8. How to agree

For an individual contributor, agreement may be recorded through the pull request process described in `docs/CLA-PROCESS.md`, including an explicit GitHub comment or an approved CLA automation that identifies this Agreement version.

For contributions owned by a company or other organization, an authorized representative must agree on behalf of that organization before a substantial Contribution is merged. The maintainer may request a separately signed copy or equivalent electronic confirmation.

A changed CLA does not retroactively replace the version You agreed to. Material changes require a new assent for future Contributions unless the earlier Agreement already authorizes them.
