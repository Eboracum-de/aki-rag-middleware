# Commercial licensing policy

AKI RAG Middleware is published as free/open-source software under **GNU AGPL-3.0-only**.

Eboracum GmbH intends to preserve the option of making the same Eboracum-owned code available under separate commercial/proprietary terms when an organization needs rights that are not compatible with its intended use of the AGPL version.

## What this does not change

A commercial license does not revoke, narrow or replace rights already granted for a version released under the AGPL. Anyone who legitimately receives an AGPL release may continue to use, study, modify and redistribute that release subject to the AGPL terms.

Accepted third-party contributions governed by the project CLA remain available in the public upstream project under AGPL-3.0-only. The CLA is a non-exclusive additional rights grant, not a copyright assignment.

The public AGPL project is not intended to be a deliberately crippled demonstration edition. Commercial licensing is a licensing alternative for organizations that need different legal terms, not a mechanism for taking previously published AGPL releases away from the community.

## Contributor rights

Contributors retain whatever copyright ownership the applicable law gives them unless a separate agreement says otherwise. Because the project may be dual-licensed, Eboracum GmbH must also have sufficient rights to use accepted contributions in both the AGPL project and separately licensed editions.

For that reason, substantial third-party contributions require the Contributor License Agreement before merge. Contributors retain ownership; see `CLA.md`, `docs/CLA-PROCESS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## No implied commercial offer

This policy records the project's licensing direction. It does not by itself constitute an offer, price list, warranty, support contract or grant of a proprietary license. Any commercial license would require a separate agreement with Eboracum GmbH.
